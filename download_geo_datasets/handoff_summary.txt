How series were judged:
- Required organism: Mus musculus.
- Required assay: bulk RNA-seq or clearly standard bulk expression profiling by high throughput sequencing.
- Excluded sc/snRNA-seq, spatial, microarray, and obvious non-bulk designs.
- Preferred one clear baseline/control and at least one perturbation condition.
- Required at least one usable comparison with >=2 biological replicates per condition.
- Strongest eligible rows had explicit count-like supplements named as counts, raw_counts, gene_count, merged_counts, HTSeq counts, or similar.
- Rows with only FPKM/TPM/log2CPM/normalized/de-results were usually excluded.
- Rows with only RAW.tar or ambiguous processed files were often kept as candidate if the biological design looked promising.

What was learned about ambiguity patterns:
- Many promising GEO series only expose RAW.tar on the series page, without an explicit gene-level count matrix.
- Many processed supplements are only FPKM, TPM, log2CPM, normalized counts, or DE result tables.
- SuperSeries often hide the usable RNA-seq contrast inside a more complex umbrella series.
- Some titles say bulk RNA-seq while the design text or supplement naming creates assay ambiguity.
- Some biologically interesting studies have enough replication but no clean baseline/control comparison.

Interpretation guidance for candidate rows:
- Candidate does not mean likely eligible by default.
- Candidate usually means one of:
  - the biological comparison looks usable but count-matrix status is unclear
  - the count files may exist only inside RAW.tar and were not opened
  - metadata are partially clear but still need sample-level confirmation
  - the study is more complex than a clean two-group analysis and needs manual reduction

Interpretation guidance for exclude rows:
- Excluded rows include true ineligible studies and also studies that failed one practical requirement for the planned workflow.
- Common exclusion reasons:
  - non-bulk assay
  - non-mouse or mixed-organism content
  - no clean control-versus-perturbation comparison
  - no usable comparison with >=2 replicates per condition
  - only normalized/non-count matrices exposed
  - metadata too ambiguous
  - outside the 2-year window relative to 2026-04-12
